BMI is a screening estimate for adults, not a diagnosis. It may not reflect body composition accurately and is not intended for children or pregnancy.

How to use this calculator

BMI is weight in kilograms divided by height in metres squared (kg/m²). For example, 70 kg at 1.75 m gives a BMI of about 22.9.

Frequently asked questions

What does BMI measure?

BMI compares weight with height. It is a screening measure, not a direct measurement of body fat.

Can children use this BMI calculator?

No. This calculator uses adult BMI categories; children require age- and sex-specific growth charts.
