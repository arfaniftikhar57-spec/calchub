Discounts, sale prices and savings

A discount reduces the original price by a stated amount or percentage. The final price is the original price minus the saving.

How to use this calculator

For a percentage discount, saving = original price × discount rate ÷ 100; final price = original price − saving.

Worked example

A £80 item with 25% off saves £20 and costs £60 before any delivery charges.

When this calculator is useful

Useful for comparing sale offers, estimating shopping budgets and checking advertised savings.

When not to rely on it

Some offers exclude items, use minimum spends or apply tax and delivery separately. Multiple discounts usually apply sequentially, not by simply adding percentages.

Practical tip

Compare the final payable amount, including postage, rather than the advertised percentage alone.

Frequently asked questions

Are two 10% discounts equal to 20% off?

No. Applied sequentially, the total reduction is 19%.

Is the original price always a fair benchmark?

Not necessarily. Check recent prices and the retailer’s terms.

Does the result include shipping?

Only if you included shipping in the amount being discounted.
