Frequently asked questions

What are maintenance calories?

They are the estimated daily calories needed to maintain your current weight.

Are these results exact?

No. Activity levels and individual metabolism vary, so these are estimates rather than medical advice.

Daily calorie needs and energy balance

This guide explains how to use the calculator, includes an example, discusses limitations and answers common questions.

Calorie calculators estimate daily energy requirements from body characteristics and activity. Actual requirements vary considerably from person to person.

How to use this calculator

Enter the requested details and choose an activity level that reflects your typical week, not your most active day. The result is an estimate of energy expenditure.

Worked example

If maintenance is estimated at 2,200 kcal per day, eating exactly that amount does not guarantee stable weight because the estimate has uncertainty.

When this calculator is useful

Useful for general meal planning and understanding how activity assumptions affect estimated energy needs.

When not to rely on it

Not appropriate as a stand-alone plan for children, pregnancy, eating disorders or medical nutrition needs. Exercise trackers and activity multipliers can be inaccurate.

Practical tip

Use longer-term observations and professional guidance when needed; avoid extreme restrictions.

Frequently asked questions

Is my calorie estimate exact?

No. It is a starting estimate based on population formulas.

Does exercise always burn the predicted calories?

No. Actual expenditure varies with intensity, body size and individual factors.

Can this replace dietitian advice?

No, particularly for medical conditions or special nutritional needs.
