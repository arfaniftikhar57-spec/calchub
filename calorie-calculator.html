How to use this calculator

This calculator estimates resting energy using the Mifflin–St Jeor equation, then multiplies it by an activity factor to estimate maintenance calories.

Frequently asked questions

What are maintenance calories?

They are the estimated daily calories needed to maintain your current weight.

Are these results exact?

No. Activity levels and individual metabolism vary, so these are estimates rather than medical advice.
