How to use this calculator

To find 20% of 150, multiply 150 by 20 and divide by 100 to get 30. Percentage change compares a new value with the original value.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find a percentage of a number?

Multiply the number by the percentage, then divide by 100.

How do I calculate percentage change?

Subtract the original value from the new value, divide by the absolute original value, then multiply by 100.
