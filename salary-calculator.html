Gross salary and hourly pay

This guide explains how to use the calculator, includes an example, discusses limitations and answers common questions.

Salary converters translate pay between hourly, weekly, monthly and yearly amounts using assumed working hours. Gross pay is before deductions.

How to use this calculator

Enter the pay rate and working hours requested. Annual gross pay is typically hourly rate × hours per week × paid weeks per year.

Worked example

At £15 per hour for 40 hours a week over 52 paid weeks, annual gross pay is £31,200.

When this calculator is useful

Useful for comparing job offers, shifts and part-time versus full-time schedules.

When not to rely on it

Actual payslips can differ due to unpaid breaks, overtime rates, holiday pay, variable shifts, tax, NI and pension contributions.

Practical tip

Compare guaranteed hours and paid breaks, not just headline hourly rates.

Frequently asked questions

Is the result take-home pay?

No. Unless stated otherwise, this is gross pay before deductions.

Does the calculation include overtime?

Only if the hours and rates entered account for overtime.

Why divide annual salary by 12?

That gives an average monthly gross amount, not necessarily each payslip.
