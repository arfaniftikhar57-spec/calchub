Converting units accurately

Unit conversions express the same physical quantity using a different measurement scale. Some units use multiplication while temperature conversions require an offset.

How to use this calculator

Choose the category, source unit and destination unit. Enter the amount and read the result. Keep units attached to every number.

Worked example

1 kilometre = 1,000 metres; 1 kilogram ≈ 2.20462 pounds; 0°C = 32°F.

When this calculator is useful

Useful for recipes, travel, science, measurements and comparing international product specifications.

When not to rely on it

Rounding can matter in engineering and medication. Similar names may refer to different units, such as US and imperial gallons.

Practical tip

Use sufficient precision for your task and double-check unit labels before copying a result.

Frequently asked questions

Can I convert metres directly to kilograms?

No. Length and mass are different physical quantities.

Why is temperature different?

Celsius and Fahrenheit use both a scale factor and an offset.

How many decimal places should I use?

Use the precision appropriate to the measurement and its purpose.
