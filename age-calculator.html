How does this age calculator work?

Enter your date of birth and the date on which you want to know your age. The calculator counts complete calendar years and months, then the remaining days. Total days are calculated from the difference between the two dates.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I calculate my age on a past date?

Yes. Choose any date on or after your date of birth.

Does this calculator account for leap years?

Yes. It uses actual calendar dates, including leap years.

Is my date of birth stored?

No. The calculation happens in your browser; this page does not send your entered dates to a server.
