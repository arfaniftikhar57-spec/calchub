UK income tax estimates

Income tax estimates can help you understand how gross annual pay may be taxed. Tax rules depend on jurisdiction, tax year and personal circumstances.

How to use this calculator

Enter annual taxable income as requested. The calculator applies its stated illustrative bands and allowances; review which UK nation and tax year the assumptions cover.

Worked example

Someone earning £35,000 does not pay the basic rate on every pound: allowances and tax bands apply to different slices of income.

When this calculator is useful

Useful for rough take-home planning and comparing gross salary scenarios.

When not to rely on it

May not cover Scottish income-tax bands, National Insurance, pension deductions, student loans, benefits, other income or changing tax codes. Tax thresholds change.

Practical tip

For actual liabilities, consult HMRC or a qualified adviser and confirm the relevant tax year.

Frequently asked questions

Is income tax the same as National Insurance?

No. They are separate deductions with different rules.

Does this cover Scotland?

Only if the calculator explicitly says so; Scottish income-tax bands differ.

Why is my payslip different?

Tax codes, pension, NI, student loans and payroll timing can affect net pay.
