How to use this calculator

For a repayment mortgage, the monthly payment depends on the amount borrowed, interest rate and number of payments. The estimate assumes the interest rate stays fixed.

Frequently asked questions

Does this include taxes and insurance?

No. The estimate covers principal and interest only, not taxes, insurance, fees or maintenance.

What happens if the interest rate changes?

Your actual payments may change. Enter a different rate to compare scenarios.
