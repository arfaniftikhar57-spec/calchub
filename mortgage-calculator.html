Mortgage payments and affordability

A mortgage calculator estimates regular repayments on a home loan. A payment estimate is not a mortgage offer or affordability assessment.

How to use this calculator

Enter the amount borrowed, annual interest rate and repayment term. For a standard repayment mortgage, the formula spreads principal and interest across monthly instalments.

Worked example

Borrowing £180,000 at a fixed 5% annual rate for 25 years gives an approximate monthly repayment of £1,052, excluding fees and insurance.

When this calculator is useful

Useful for comparing house-price scenarios, deposits, mortgage terms and the effect of interest-rate changes.

When not to rely on it

Actual products may include fees, introductory rates, variable rates, overpayment rules and affordability checks. Interest-only mortgages work differently.

Practical tip

Compare total interest as well as monthly payments, and leave room in your budget for maintenance, insurance and taxes.

Frequently asked questions

Does the payment include council tax?

No. Council tax and other household running costs are separate.

Will my lender offer the displayed rate?

Not necessarily. Rates and eligibility depend on the lender and your circumstances.

Why does a longer term cost more?

A longer term usually lowers each payment but increases total interest paid.
